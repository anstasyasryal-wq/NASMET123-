# Implementation Plan - 10 Dedicated Standalone Screens & Joyful Restyling

## 1. Overview & Goals
- Group application features into **10 dedicated standalone primary screens**, each opening independently when navigated to.
- Screen 1 remains the standalone **Home (`home`)** dashboard featuring the spiritual wheel and welcoming greeting.
- Restyle the application aesthetic to be **welcoming, joyful, luminous, and engaging** (vibrant warm gold, joyful Coptic accents, smooth transitions, and polished welcoming banners).
- Update navigation (Header, Bottom Nav, and Sidebar Drawer) to cleanly route to these 10 distinct screens.

## 2. The 10 Dedicated Screens Structure
1. **الرئيسية (`home`)**: The standalone dashboard with the spiritual wheel, daily inspiration, and quick entry cards.
2. **المسابقات والمراحل (`competitions_hub`)**: Competitions, stages, and interactive quizzes.
3. **حصن الصلاة اليومي (`prayer_fortress`)**: Daily psalm fortress, prayer reminders, and spiritual shields.
4. **حصالة الفضائل (`virtue_jar`)**: Virtue logging, spiritual points, and progress tracking.
5. **المكتبة والآباء (`library`)**: Patristic library, spiritual books, and search.
6. **الحياة الروحية (`spiritual_life`)**: Inner chamber, spiritual journal, and confession preparation.
7. **المشاركون والتحديات (`collective_challenges` / `participants`)**: Community leaderboards, participant list, and collective challenges.
8. **التقويم الكنسي (`spiritual_calendar`)**: Liturgical calendar, feasts, and church seasons.
9. **الألعاب الروحية (`spiritual_games`)**: Engaging spiritual games, fortune wheel, and interactive tests.
10. **دستور المكرس (`consecrated_manual`)**: Spiritual guide, monastic oasis, and foundational manuals.

## 3. UI/UX & Joyful Restyling Steps
- Enhance color harmony with warm luminous amber-gold, soft glowing gradients, and welcoming greeting headers across all views.
- Ensure zero broken links and fully working interactive handlers for each screen.
- Verify mobile responsiveness and bottom navigation integration.
